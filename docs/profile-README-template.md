### What I build

Products at the intersection of **AI agents** and **crypto payments**, on Solana
and the x402 / USDC stack. I care about systems whose claims can be checked
rather than trusted, and about getting the boring parts right: honest fills,
integer money, on-chain records that cannot be revised.
